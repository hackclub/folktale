Frequently asked questions

Who is this for?

Anyone 13 through 18 years old inclusive who wants to get into the magical art of programming!

Is this really free?

Yup! This is run through Hack Club, a 501(c) nonprofit whose mission is to spread programming awareness as far as possible.

What is the AI policy for Hack Club Folktale?

Since this is a specifically beginner-focused program, we ask that you refrain from all AI usage, as it robs the productive struggle that is learning.
